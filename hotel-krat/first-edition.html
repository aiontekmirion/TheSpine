Lies of P reimagines Pinocchio in a city transformed by mechanical invention and disaster. Its story asks what makes a person, what obedience conceals, and whether tenderness can survive the machinery around it.

This companion offers a short original introduction and a route to the dialogue, recorded scenes, and official art. It is a guide to those sources, rather than a reproduction of the game’s script.

A short reading path

From an awakening to a choice.

Read the opening freely. The later notes reveal major story developments.

01 A voice, a city, a door

A puppet wakes in a devastated Krat. Sophia’s voice guides him toward Hotel Krat, where a gathering of survivors offers a foothold in the city. The first threshold already poses a question: who gets to enter a place of safety?

Follow the transcript’s Chapter I, from Krat Central Station to the hotel. Notice the difference between the welcome offered by a person and the rules enforced at the door.

SPOILERS · 02 Read the rules twice

The search through Krat gradually unsettles the explanations supplied by its authorities. Commands intended to guarantee safety do not settle who is responsible for harm. Later revelations make earlier encounters worth revisiting.

Reading route: the Grand Covenant discussion at the hotel, Venigni’s investigation, and the King of Puppets’ message. Keep first impressions beside later evidence.

SPOILERS · 03 A person beyond a purpose

The central journey reaches a conflict between a creator’s wishes and the identity of his creation. The ending branches; no single outcome should silently stand for all of them.

Follow the transcript’s Chapter XI through Sophia’s story and the final choices. Read each labelled alternative separately before comparing what changes.

SPOILERS · 04 Return through Overture

Overture returns to Krat’s past. Read it after the base story if you want earlier relationships and losses to acquire their later significance.

The companion transcript follows Lea and Romeo, with other encounters along the way. Rosaura’s recognition of a new friend offers a particularly useful question: can resemblance matter without making two people the same?

Beyond the main quest

Follow a person, not a boss fight.

Polendina
SPOILER-FREE INTRODUCTION

A butler at the hotel. Begin with the welcome, then follow his conversations with and about Antonia. Read the deeper note below only when you want the destination of that thread.

MAJOR SPOILERS The concierge who remembers

Polendina’s attachment to Antonia moves the question of puppet personhood into ordinary care. Her death makes memory itself painful; he chooses to erase his awakened identity. The desk remains, but the meaning of the person behind it has changed.

Reading route: Chapter VI, Hotel Krat for his confession; the later hotel conversations for Antonia’s treatment and his farewell. Preserve the treatment branches when comparing them.

Aion Tekmirion’s reading: this arc makes the concierge a compelling figure for a world concerned with memory and welcome. The “unsent letter” discussed in our concierge concept is our proposed addition; it has not been verified as part of Polendina’s dialogue.

Gemini

A travelling voice and guide. Follow what he remembers, what he cannot explain, and the way companionship lives inside practical directions.

Sophia

The voice that reaches the traveller. Follow her account across the game, allowing later disclosures to change your understanding of the beginning.
