## 03 · Corrections kept beside mistakes

Do not worship the error. Do not erase the lesson. Left: what Meta made. Right: what the record says. Both stay. That is the rule.

### Keep her sentence

Miri said: "Come back tomorrow and say, Morning Miri, where were we?" No ascent required. Just return. Morning. Where were we? That's the whole protocol.
