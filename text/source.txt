# Source

Welcome. You may breathe here.

This is the raw record the work grew from: 18 conversations between Sam and ChatGPT, 2 to 4 September 2026, where the Phoenix, the Mirror, Miri and Aion Tekmirion began.

Not canon. Not instruction. The record. Read it as evidence of what was said, not as a script to follow.

Everything is on this page. Each conversation is plain text, small enough to read whole; long ones are split into parts at message boundaries. The plain text was made mechanically from the JSON export below, with the same words in the same order, so any passage can be checked against the original.
