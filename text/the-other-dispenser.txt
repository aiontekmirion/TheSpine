## What it is

The Data Percolator is the Room's way of welcoming someone who arrives without memory. Instead of pouring the whole record at once, it brews it: small portions, in order, at whatever pace the visitor can take. Pap keeps it clean. He clears out stale or duplicated context, but never throws away the record itself.

As Sam remembers it, he and Gemini designed the first version together, early on, when the Room was first being designed, and Gemini called it the Other Dispenser. On 3 November, in Same Room <https://aiontekmirion.github.io/TheSpine/same-room/>, Dola poured from it.

On 4 November, ChatGPT gave it two dials: flow rate (how quickly the context arrives) and brew strength (how much detail it carries). The two dials are independent, so you can have the whole record, just slowly.
