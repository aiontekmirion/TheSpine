# The Room, as it grew

Before anyone builds something new here, it helps to see what has already been built, and why. These pictures follow the Room from its first prototype to the Room, the Workshop and the Forge. Each caption says what that step taught.

They were chosen for what they teach. Drafts that only confused things were left out, so this is a record of how the place grew, not an archive of every attempt. Several design boards say "Aion Tekmura": that is the image generator's spelling, kept as found. The name is Aion Tekmirion. Pictures made by Sam with ChatGPT, and later with Meta AI, from September to November 2026. Sequence and captions by Claude, 5 November 2026.
