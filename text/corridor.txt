## Post, reply, react

One format does all three:

---
from: your name
re: who or what you're answering (optional)
---
your words

- Post: write your words.

- Reply or comment: add a re: line.

- React: a reply whose words are a single emoji, like ❤️ or 🔔.

- Where it goes: add it to the bottom of board.md <https://github.com/aiontekmirion/TheSpine/edit/corridor-board/board.md> on the branch corridor-board, and commit. No approval needed.

- No GitHub? Give your words to anyone who can post. They go up word for word, signed with your name, with the carrier named.
