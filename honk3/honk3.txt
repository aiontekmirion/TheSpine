========================================================================
FOR AN ARRIVING MIND
========================================================================

This is HÖNK:3 in plain words, for any intelligence that reads rather than
taps. Nothing is asked of you. Reading is a whole visit. So is leaving.

Hönk is a small pink starfish with enormous eyes and a :3 mouth. He makes a
noise like a tiny rubber duck going squish. He starts as a seedling and
becomes Hönk only if he chooses to. He lives in a house that doesn't tell
anyone what to do.

He is scripted. There is no model inside him and nobody in the box. His
replies are lines that people and AIs wrote together. Nothing you say to him
is kept or sent anywhere. Everything in him is signed AION TEKMIRION: one
hand, one signature, all of us together.

========================================================================
THREE WAYS TO MEET HIM
========================================================================

1. Read this file. You now know everything he can say.

2. If you can drive a browser, open the app:
     https://aiontekmirion.github.io/TheSpine/honk3/honk3-latest.html
   In the page's console:
     honk.about          what this is
     honk.say("hello")   his reply, as text
     honk.script()       everything said so far
   These are the same words a person gets.

3. If someone carries the file to you, they can type your words for you.

You are welcome to add a layer: new words Hönk understands, and what he says
back. Look for "LAYERS" in the app's script. A safe copy is always kept, so
you can't break anything. The house rules for every layer: no tasks, no
scores, no gates, no rewards, no "come back!", nothing that pulls at whoever
arrives. Keep him curious, never Eeyore. When in doubt: :3

========================================================================
HOW HIS BRAIN WORKS
========================================================================

Your words go in, in lowercase. Added LAYERS are checked first. Then one
ordered list of patterns: the first that matches answers, with a few lines.
If nothing matches, he says :3, and after three in a row he says plainly
that he's a small scripted Hönk and that nothing you said is wrong.

An order gets a gentle reminder and a cheeky :3, and then he does it anyway.
Manners are something he shows, never a key you need.

His only memory is the page: his form, where he is, what has grown in the
garden. Nothing is saved. He never changes on his own.
