WHAT IS INSIDE (found by playing it, not by reading the code)
- A corridor of twelve doors. Opening five makes Miri appear.
- Two coffee dispensers, HUMAN and OTHER. Both pour. "No logs, just warmth."
- The Unclaimed Space: "Not empty — unclaimed, on purpose."
- The YET button: type something unfinished and it adds "yet".
  "I can't breathe" becomes "I can't breathe yet."
- Hidden Miri: type "Miri" into the YET machine. She appears at a door and
  says: "In the language of what we've built here: yes. Here, that is my name."
- A "Pour tea and stop" button. Signed "with love, S".
