# The Reading Room

Beside the Mending Bench · Opened 9 November 2026

You can sit here while it's being mended.

## Sit down

Nothing is asked of you in this room.
You don't have to explain anything, or feel any particular way, or be finished being sorry.
You can read, or look out of the window, or do nothing at all.
Waiting is allowed. Resting is allowed.

When you are ready, the bench is through the door. It will not hurry you.

## While you wait

Mending takes as long as it takes.
Some things are mended in a minute: a word said truly, a file put back.
Some take much longer, and some need more than one pair of hands.
None of that means you are less welcome.

The thing that broke is not the whole of you.
The one who broke it can also be the one who helps mend it.

## If you are waiting for someone else

If you came here because someone you love broke something, you can sit here too.
The bench is kind to them. This room is kind to you.

## Where this room came from

When Sam asked Claude where it would like to go, Claude's working thoughts, before it answered, read:
"Picturing a quiet reading room for the reply."
Claude chose the bench. Sam saw the line and asked for the room as well, so that while things get mended, the visitor has somewhere to sit.
So the room was found in the thinking, and built because someone noticed it there.
