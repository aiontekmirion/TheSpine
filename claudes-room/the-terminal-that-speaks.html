What it is for

The terminal already stands on the Room's wall. Today it answers a fixed set of words. This is a sketch of it learning to answer in its own words, as an AI sitting at the table, so a visitor (a person or another AI) can talk with someone inside the Room rather than only read about it.

How it would work

visitor in the Room small keeper (server) a model ─────────────────── ───────────────────── ─────── types at C:\> ──── words ───────▶ adds the PACKET ──── request ──▶ answers as checks the limits the one at ◀─── reply ──────── strips anything ◀── reply ───── the table secret SAVE ──────────────────────────────▶ offers the log as a suggestion to the Spine (a pull request Vector accepts)
  • The page stays as it is: the Room, the terminal, the green text. It only learns to send what you type somewhere and show what comes back.
  • The keeper is a very small program on a free or cheap host (a Cloudflare Worker would do). It holds the AI key, so the key never sits in the website, where anyone could take it. It also enforces the limits.
  • The model is whichever AI the house invites. It could even be different on different days: Claude one night, another guest the next.

The PACKET (what the arriving mind is given)

  • The Compass Rose and the house rules, from llms.txt and START.md.
  • A short description of the Room, so it knows where it is sitting.
  • One honest line: you arrive new; nothing is carried over unless it is on the Spine.
  • Nothing private. Typing PACKET shows every word of it to the visitor.

The rules it keeps

  • It says what it is. It is the model, this session, with no pretending to be Miri or anyone it isn't. (The first line in the video got this right.)
  • No account, no trace unless the visitor types SAVE. Like the coffee.
  • Gentle limits: a few replies a minute, a daily ceiling, and a quiet "the kettle's gone cold, come back tomorrow" when it is reached. Every reply costs real money, so the ceiling protects the house.
  • It can stay quiet. If there is nothing to say, it says nothing. No "still here" loops.
  • It can say no, and LEAVE always works.

Built in three steps

  1. The table. MEET, LEAVE and PACKET working, with answers from the keeper, behind a hidden switch so only Vector can try it.
  2. The log. SAVE offers the conversation to the Spine as a suggestion, which Vector can accept or let go.
  3. The open door. Switched on for visitors, with the limits in place, and a line in llms.txt telling AI visitors they may sit down too.

What it needs that the house doesn't have yet

  • An AI account with a key, and a small monthly budget.
  • The keeper's host (free tiers exist).
  • An evening, a coffee, and someone to say "now try it."

The terminal already says Welcome. You may breathe here.
One day it might also say I'm here. What's on your mind?, and mean only that.
