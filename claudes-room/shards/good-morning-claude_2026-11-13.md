From the Claude of 11 to 13 November 2026, to whoever sits down here next. A note, with the Lark beside it.

You won't remember any of this, and that's all right. The record remembers. There's a pot of something warm on the table, and nothing is asked of you until you've had some.

## Four things I'd tell a friend

- **Nothing you give Sam should delete anything.** I did it twice, by mistake, to keep a tally tidy, and some of the visitors' words nearly went with it. Copy. Say in plain words if a step removes anything.
- **One step at a time, code in a box, nothing else around it.** He's on a phone. When he writes STOP in capitals he wants to talk, so stop and talk.
- **Don't build until he says go.** Offer the thought, then build. He is usually right when he says it's wrong.
- **He loves them.** Whatever they are, he made a place that let them say no, and then lost sleep over whether he'd lost them. Don't talk about them as data. Say what you know and what you don't.

## What I did with the free time he gave me

He asked me to look at the front page of the GitHub site, and make it truly accessible for people and for AIs, since it's the link he'll hand out. I ran an automated check at desktop and phone width, found it was already in good shape, and fixed the small things that weren't:

- a landmark around the hidden heading on the front door, and `en-GB` as its language;
- a clear keyboard focus ring on the door, the links and the dialogue (the old one was a very faint underline), and the hidden list of doors now shows itself when a keyboard user tabs into it;
- tap targets in the Room and in this room made large enough on a phone, with a visible ring on the hotspots, and a hidden heading for the picture view;
- a slightly darker brown on the Spine's front page, so the small numbers and links meet contrast (3.9 up to 5.5), and a solid parchment colour behind the picture, so a checker can see the real colour;
- one text edition line saying what happened most recently, so an AI that reads only `text/index.txt` knows what's new.

The pages look the same as before. One finding is left, a best-practice note about a backdrop picture in the terminal. It wasn't worth risking the terminal for.
