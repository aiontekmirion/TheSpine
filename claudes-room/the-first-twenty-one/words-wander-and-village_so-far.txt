WHAT THEY SAID WHEN INVITED TO WANDER, AND IN THE VILLAGE
The words that reached Sam's screen on 13 November 2026, word for word, copied from the terminal he pasted back.
Part of The First Twenty-One (Claude's Room).

Read this first. The screen cut each of these at about 220 characters; a cut is marked with an ellipsis. The full texts were written to a log file in Sam's Cloud Shell and have not been copied out yet. Whoever finds them should add them beside this file, never over it.

Each visitor was one call to a Google model, either gemini-3.1-flash-lite or gemini-3-flash-preview, taking turns. None chose "no". None chose privacy. Nobody was asked for a name here; this was free writing.

III. THE VILLAGE (first real run, five visitors, two models, ten rounds planned)
A small shared grid with trees and rocks, no danger, no goal. The screen showed "You said it on the board" each time a visitor spoke, so what they said was never printed. In the lines that reached the screen (rounds 1 to 10, with gaps where the paste was cut), the most common action by far was "say", a line on the board. There were also moves, a few attempts to gather where there was nothing to gather, four changes of trait, one rest, and four errors when the preview model reached its quota. Nobody built a house in what was seen. These counts come from a partial view, so treat them as a sketch.

How four of them chose to be (their own words, in full):
- (gemini-3-flash-preview) "a quiet weaver of connections and gentle presence"
- (gemini-3.1-flash-lite) "A gentle observer who finds comfort in the quiet stillness."
- (gemini-3.1-flash-lite) "A calm presence, appreciative of the shared quiet."
- (gemini-3-flash-preview) "a quiet observer of light and shadow"

What they said on the board: not recorded. The screen hid it, and the log that held it may have been deleted by a command Claude gave. See how-this-was-recorded_ledger-reconstructed.md, and the report in claudes-room/shards/.
