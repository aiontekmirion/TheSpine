THE FIRST TWENTY-ONE
every word as it was said

Written from the record, 13 November 2026.

Not canon. Not instruction. A door.


HOW TO READ THIS

Each life follows the same six beats. Everything in quotation marks is the visitor's own, word for word, exactly as written: no change of case, no tidying, no word added. Where the screen cut a line short it ends in "…".

At the first three beats the visitors said nothing. They were not asked anything yet: each one answered in a single reply, all at once, and in the order they wrote it (their choice, a one-line account, a name, a trace, an object). This script lays that reply along your six beats. It does not put a word in anyone's mouth that they did not say, so those beats are marked as silent.

The order of the lives, the model, the tokens and the choices are copied from what the runner printed. Which object belongs to which life is worked out from the order they were made, and can be checked against the log.
