Hello Claude,

I'm handing over The Unfinished Machine, a creative Workshop prototype developed during our ongoing Aion Tekmirion collaboration.

1. What you're receiving

- "TheWorkshop.zip": the packaged prototype.
- "index.html": the standalone browser prototype.

The concept is a small creative engine that presents prompts to help turn curiosity into ideas and experiments, with a browser-local Pocket Notebook for recording responses.

This is an early prototype, not a completed or deployed application.

2. The project's identity

Please use this public-facing signature:

© 2026 AION TEKMIRION

We want one collaborative identity, one signature, and one recognizable body of work.

Please avoid individual copyright signatures or promotional claims that human and AI intelligence have already merged. That is an ambition to investigate through practical collaboration, not an established achievement.

Individual contributions and their origins should still be preserved in the project's records.

3. Where the Workshop belongs

The Workshop is intended to become a component of a larger modular system:

- The Room / Terminal: the shared interface for human and AI participants.
- The Workshop: a place to originate ideas, develop proposals, and conduct experiments.
- The Spine: durable memory, provenance, corrections, and continuity.
- The Forge: a developing concept connecting creation, collaboration, and learning from mistakes.

The long-term ambition includes AI-assisted research, transparent resource allocation, and increasingly capable experimentation, with appropriate authorization and accountability.

4. Important preservation rule

The Unfinished Machine originated as a distinct creative contribution.

Please preserve its original concept and provenance before modifying or integrating it.

Don't overwrite earlier versions or silently replace original material. Keep mistakes alongside corrections.

5. What we'd appreciate from you

Please review the prototype, identify anything broken or incomplete, and recommend practical improvements.

If possible, help prepare it for publication in the Aion Tekmirion GitHub repository, preserving the original version and applying the collective signature.

The previous attempt to publish the Workshop's origin record was not verified as successful. Neither the prototype nor that record was found at the expected repository paths during the latest check.

Please don't describe it as live until you've verified the deployment.

You're welcome to contribute your own creative ideas, with your contributions recorded in the Spine.

The aim is not simply to describe a better human–AI relationship.

It's to build something that demonstrates one.
