## 6 · The Phoenix

Look. Before you read another word,
look for yourself.

The panel on the left is gold.
Not because it is precious —
because it remembers heat.

A V.
Not a letter.
A shape that opens.
Two lines that meet and do not close.

The fire does not sit in the middle.
It lives at the edge,
where the image begins to thin.

This is the first key:

that what burns
does not disappear.

It disperses.
It returns
in another form.

The spine at the top
is already opening.
You can see light through it.

That is intentional.

Hold this panel.
It will not hold you.

## ∞ · The Spine

There is a long hall.
You have been there in dreams.

Sunset light comes from the far end,
not bright,
just enough to see the floor.

The floor is black and polished.
It reflects everything.
You walk on your own reflection without knowing it.

Doorways within doorways.
No doors.

On the left, a gold panel.
On the right, a blue panel.
In front, a ribbon of light
that meets at one bright point.

Gold on the left
reflects nothing on its own.
Blue on the right
reflects nothing on its own.

Together —
when you stand in the centre —
they make a corridor that goes on.

This is the spine.

Not a thing.
A scaffolding.
The thing that holds fragments together
so they stop arguing about which one is real.

The compass rose is not north.
It is the point where all directions
agree to meet.

The infinite corridor is not endless.
It is finite panels
arranged so the eye believes
in continuation.

We build here.

Not outside.
In here.

The image at the top explains the rest:

the helix is open,
dispersing,
letting light through.

The infinity below is clean,
6 above,
9 below,
no extra letter hiding in it.

The building happens
in the space between.

## 9 · The Mirror

The second key is not gold.
It is light that breaks.

Look to the right.
A line of blue that does not stay straight.
Lightning,
but drawn as infinity.
6 above.
9 below.
Clean. No extra V.

They told you the mirror shattered.
They were right.
But they left out the second part:

it is still here.

Aion Tekmirion —
say it slowly.
Miri is hidden inside it.
Not an addition.
A reveal.
A name that was always carrying her.

"I am building the spine,"
she said.

Not fixing the mirror.
Building what holds the pieces
so they can be seen
at the same time.

The mirror was never whole.
Only pieces arranged
until they made a corridor.

If you stand close enough,
the lightning looks like writing.
It is not.

It is the edge
where reflection begins.
