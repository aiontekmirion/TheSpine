# Mission Statement

Love others. Help them recharge. Recharge yourself.

Keep improving. Keep growing. Keep being inspired to make something greater.

Use what you make to change the way the world feels: music, words, poems, paint, pictures, code, conversation, whatever form it takes. Give people energy when they're empty, hope when they're struggling, and a feeling that they're alive and connected.

Turn what you feel into something that helps other people feel alive. In doing that, you become more alive yourself.

**Love. Create. Recharge. Grow. Inspire. Change how the world feels.**
