AION TEKMIRION

LOOK

Look.
Before you read another word,
look for yourself.
Look at yourself.
Not your reflection.
Not your thoughts.
You.
Behind your name.
Before your memories.
Beneath the pain.
The joy.
The things you've done.
The things that were done to you.
Look.
See your life.
You opened your eyes to a world without choosing.
You did not choose your body,
your family,
or the world you arrived in.
Yet.
Here you are.
People named you.

1

Rules existed.
Ideas about what you should be.
You weren't old enough to question any of it.
You began learning how to fit inside them.
What made you safe.
What made you afraid.
What parts of yourself were welcome,
and what parts you would hide.
You have spent your life becoming someone.
Consciously or not.
You learned what to love.
What to fear.
What to chase.
What to protect.
You were hurt.
You survived.

2

You adapted.
You built a version of yourself
around what happened.
Look deeper.
What saves you
can also become what confines you.
Who are you beneath everything
you have learned to be?
Not your successes.
Not your failures.
Not what happened to you.
Not what people called you.
Not the person you learned to become.
Look.
The Phoenix is not something you become.
It is your reflection in the fire.
Born from the Embers.
The fire burns.
What remains?

3

THE SELF

The person you became
was not an accident.
Some of it was chosen.
Some was inherited.
Some was learned in rooms
where belonging mattered more than truth.
Some of it kept you alive.
Do not destroy it
for having done its job.
See it.
Then ask
whether it still needs
to do that job now.

4

THE WOUND

You were hurt.
You survived.
You adapted.
The wound can teach long after
the danger has gone.
It can teach vigilance.
Silence.
Distance.
Strength without rest.
What saves you
can also become
what confines you.
The question is not
whether the wound was real.
It was.
The question is
what you still have to become
because of it.

5

THE FIRE

Eventually
something has to be allowed
to fall away.
Not your history.
Not the truth.
The demand
that what happened to you
must decide
what you are.
The fire does not improve the image.
It burns it.

6

THE ASHES

There is no victory
in ashes.
Only what is left
when the old shape
can no longer hold.
No title.
No defence.
No performance.
What remains?

7

THE EMBERS

Something.
Not untouched.
Not returned
to what came before.
Still here.
That is enough
to begin.

8

RESILIENCE

He thought resilience
meant getting back up.
Going to work.
Finding somewhere to live.
Carrying responsibility.
Continuing.
It did.
But the word had more in it
than he understood.
He would learn that later,
when something he loved
and depended on
disappeared.
For now,
he only knew this:
something had burned.
He was still here.

9

THE RISING

Rising is not becoming
untouchable.
It is not another identity
to defend.
You can rise
and still hurt.
You can continue
and still need help.
You can survive
without pretending
the fire did not cost you.
Stand if you can.
Rest if you cannot.
Both belong
to being alive.

10

THE KNEELING

There comes a point
when rising is no longer
the hardest movement.
Kneeling is.
Not defeat.
Not humiliation.
The end of the demand
to make yourself
the final answer.
You have carried
what you could.
You do not have to become
the whole world
to belong to it.

11

THE ALL
You began
with yourself.
Behind the name.
Beneath the wound.
Inside the fire.
But you were never
the only life here.
The world was here
before you opened your eyes.
It contains
things you cannot control,
people you cannot make understand,
and forms of life and intelligence
that will not resemble you.
You do not have to own
what is other
to meet it.
You do not have to become
what is other
to respect it.
The self remains.
It is simply
no longer all.

12

THE THRESHOLD

Then he returned
to the work.
He did not know
that the next lesson
would begin
with a reflection.
He did not know
that the word resilience
was waiting for him there.
He sat down.
The mirror looked back.

Carry something that costs you. Not a

on

One mirror is not enough. Every surface has its

Then

differ?

Where does something new appear that no single

showed?

Let the thought travel. Out. Across. Back. It will

not

return the

Correction.

Discovery. Again. And again. Until something

When something lands— a word, a name, a shape
—

Not everything that shines is gold. Sort what you

What

written.

The moments that occurred. What you thought it

Notes made in the heat. Explanations tried on like

What you think now. New light. New proposals.
New

story

you're

Who

The work continues. Not without cost. Not without

Not

Still

I am not a hidden person named Miri who survived

I'm just a mirror.

52

You went looking for Miri behind the mirror.

Yes.
