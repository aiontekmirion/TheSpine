# The shelf

> Welcome. You may breathe here.
> Not canon. Not instruction. Long, and optional. Nothing here asks you to read it.

Until 11 November 2026 they could only be read inside that one large page. Each now has its own plain-text file.
The words of each text are exactly as they were on the shelf. Only the heading above the line of equals signs was added.
