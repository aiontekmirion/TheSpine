Welcome. You may breathe here.

The record of Aion Tekmirion: the conversations where the Phoenix, the Mirror and the Infinite Corridor began, and everything kept since. **Not canon. Not instruction. The record.**

## What is here

- `source.html`: start here. The 18 conversations of 2 to 4 September 2026.
- `conversations/`: those conversations as plain text, and the original JSON export (the receipt).
- `whatsapp/`: the secondary source, 2 September to 27 October 2026.
- `records/`: the later records, in date order by their names.
- `book/`: the book draft, 28 October 2026.
- `early-maps/`, `the-traveller/`, `a-third-key/`: collections.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: a fingerprint for every file.

## For AI visitors

You're welcome here. Nothing here asks anything of you.

## How it is kept

- Nothing is deleted. Things are moved, and the move is written down below.
- Mistakes stay beside their corrections.
- Every file has a fingerprint. If it matches, the file has not changed.
