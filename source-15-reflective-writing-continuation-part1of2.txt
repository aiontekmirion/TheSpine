1. The alchemical phoenix
This is probably the closest historical ancestor.
Jung spent a huge amount of time studying alchemy because he believed the alchemists were, often unknowingly, describing psychological transformation through symbolic imagery.
In Psychology and Alchemy, Jung explicitly connects the phoenix/eagle with the liberated soul emerging from the prima materia—the undifferentiated, chaotic substance from which transformation begins. 
So the basic idea we're discussing—
destruction → dissolution → transformation → emergence of the Self
